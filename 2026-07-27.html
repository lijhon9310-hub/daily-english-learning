clarify /ˈklærəfaɪ/

记忆 词根 clar-（清楚，如 clear）+ -ify（使…化）= 使清楚 → 澄清。
briefing /ˈbriːfɪŋ/
简报；情况介绍会
例句 The manager gave a short briefing before the client call.
记忆 brief（简短的）+ -ing（名词）= 简短说明会 → 简报。
concise /kənˈsaɪs/
简洁的；简明的
例句 Keep the email concise — busy people skim, they don't read.
记忆 con-（完全）+ cis（切，如 scissors 剪刀）= 切掉多余 → 简洁。
delegate /ˈdelɪɡeɪt/
委派；授权（给他人任务）
例句 Good leaders delegate, they don't try to do everything alone.
记忆 de-（向下）+ leg（派遣，如 legacy）+ -ate = 派下去 → 委派。
feedback /ˈfiːdbæk/
反馈；意见
例句 Could I get your feedback on the draft before Friday?
记忆 feed（喂）+ back（回）= 喂回来的信息 → 反馈。
consensus /kənˈsensəs/
共识；一致意见
例句 We reached a consensus to launch in the second quarter.
记忆 con-（共同）+ sens（感觉，如 sense）+ -us = 共同的感觉 → 共识。
escalate /ˈeskəleɪt/
上报；升级（把问题交更高层处理）
例句 If the bug isn't fixed by noon, escalate it to the platform team.
记忆 来自 escalator（自动扶梯，逐级而上）→ 把问题一级级往上送。
correspond /ˌkɒrəˈspɒnd/
通信；相符；与…一致
例句 Please correspond with the client and confirm the schedule.
记忆 cor-（共同）+ respond（回应）= 一来一往地回应 → 通信/相符。
articulate /ɑːˈtɪkjuleɪt/
清晰表达；口齿清楚
例句 She articulated the plan so well that nobody had questions.
记忆 articul-（关节/清晰发音）+ -ate = 把话"咬合"清楚 → 清晰表达。
follow up /ˌfɒləʊ ˈʌp/
跟进；后续落实
例句 I'll follow up with a summary email after the meeting.
记忆 follow（跟随）+ up（向上）= 跟进行动。沟通后的"跟进"决定结果。

邮件先讲目的（to keep everyone aligned）再给议程；用 numbered list 让结构一目了然；结尾 keep + 动词体现轻量礼貌。

听力材料一 · 团队每日站会（任务澄清与委派）

🎬 职场日常站会，经理简短复盘、分配任务并上报阻碍。

Morning, everyone. Let's keep this stand-up short. First, a quick recap: we shipped the new onboarding flow on Friday, and early feedback is positive. Sarah, could you own the follow-up survey and close it by Thursday? Thanks. Mark, please draft the client briefing — keep it concise, two pages max. One blocker: the API latency issue. I'll escalate it to the platform team this afternoon. Anything else before we wrap? Great, let's sync again tomorrow.

听力任务

  1. 经理把"后续调研"交给谁，要求何时完成？答：交给 Sarah，要求周四前（by Thursday）完成。
  2. 给客户的简报（briefing）有什么格式要求？答：要简洁（concise），最多两页（two pages max）。
  3. 经理提到遇到了什么阻碍（blocker），打算如何处理？答：API 延迟问题，计划当天下午上报平台团队（escalate it to the platform team）。

重点词汇

  • stand-up — 站立会议；每日短会（敏捷团队常用）
  • recap — recapitulation 缩写，简要回顾
  • ship — （产品/功能）发布、上线
  • own — 口语中作动词，意为"负责、主导"
  • escalate — 上报；把问题升级给更高层级

听力材料二 · 一对一面谈中的建设性反馈

🎬 经理与下属 Leo 的一对一，就演示表现给出反馈与建议。

Thanks for making time, Leo. I wanted to share some feedback on last week's presentation. Overall it was clear and well-structured. That said, a few points could be more articulate — try to slow down when explaining the metrics. Also, invite questions earlier so the room stays engaged. I really value your inputs, and I think with these tweaks you'll be even stronger. How does that sound?

听力任务

  1. 经理对 Leo 上次演示的总体评价如何？答：清晰且结构良好（clear and well-structured）。
  2. 经理建议 Leo 在哪方面更"articulate"？答：解释数据指标时放慢语速（slow down when explaining the metrics）。
  3. 经理建议他更早做什么来保持听众参与？答：更早邀请提问（invite questions earlier），让现场保持投入。

重点词汇

  • constructive feedback — 建设性反馈；有助改进的评语
  • articulate — 清晰表达；把话说到位
  • engaged — 投入的；专注参与的
  • inputs — 此处指"意见、贡献"，职场常用复数
  • tweaks — 微调；小改进
