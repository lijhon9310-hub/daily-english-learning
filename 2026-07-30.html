symptom/ˈsɪmptəm/
n.
症状（患者主观感受）
"A persistent cough can be a symptom of a respiratory infection."
记忆：sym(共同)+ptom(发生) → 身体"共同发出"的异常信号 → 症状。对比 sign（体征，医生查出）vs symptom（症状，患者感受）。
diagnosis/ˌdaɪəɡˈnoʊsɪs/
n.
诊断
"The doctor's diagnosis was acute bronchitis."
记忆：dia(穿过)+gnos(知道)+is → 彻底"弄明白"病因 → 诊断。词根 gno 同 know（知道），如 recognize（认出）。
prescription/prɪˈskrɪpʃn/
n.
处方，药方
"Take this prescription to the pharmacy to get your medicine."
记忆：pre(前)+script(写)+ion → 提前"写下来"的用药指令 → 处方。script 表"写"，如 manuscript（手稿）。
appointment/əˈpɔɪntmənt/
n.
预约，约定
"I have a dental appointment at three this afternoon."
记忆：ap+point(指定)+ment → 被"指定"的时间点 → 预约。职场也指职位任命（如 get an appointment）。
insurance/ɪnˈʃʊrəns/
n.
保险
"Does your health insurance cover this procedure?"
记忆：in(使)+sure(确定)+ance → 让未来"确定"有保障 → 保险。sure 同源 secure（安全的）。
recover/rɪˈkʌvər/
v.
康复，恢复
"She is slowly recovering from the flu."
记忆：re(再)+cover(取回) → 把健康重新"取回" → 康复。反义：relapse（复发）。
prevention/prɪˈvenʃn/
n.
预防
"Prevention is better than cure, so wash your hands often."
记忆：pre(前)+vent(来)+ion → 在问题"到来之前"挡住 → 预防。vent 同 come（来），如 adventure。
chronic/ˈkrɑːnɪk/
adj.
慢性的
"He manages a chronic back pain with regular exercise."
记忆：chron(时间)+ic → 长时间持续的 → 慢性的。对比 acute（急性的）。词根 chron 同 clock（时间）。
vaccine/ˈvæksiːn/
n.
疫苗
"The flu vaccine is recommended every autumn."
记忆：vacc(牛)+ine → 源自牛痘（cowpox）的发明 → 疫苗。联想 inoculation（接种）。
well-being/ˈwel biːɪŋ/
n.
身心健康，幸福感
"Employee well-being should be a priority for every company."
记忆：well(好)+being(状态) → 良好的状态 → 身心健康。职场高频词：employee well-being（员工福祉）。

① 邮件 Email 申请病假

Subject: Sick Leave Request for July 31

Dear Ms. Garcia,
I am writing to request a sick day tomorrow, July 31, as I have come down with a fever and a bad cough. I will submit the leave form in the HR system and stay reachable by email for urgent matters. I plan to resume work on Monday and have handed today's tasks over to Tom. Thank you for your understanding.
Best regards,
Kevin Wu
主题：申请 7 月 31 日病假
尊敬的加西亚女士：我写信申请明天（7 月 31 日）休病假，因我发烧并剧烈咳嗽。我会在 HR 系统提交请假单，紧急事宜可通过邮件联系我。我计划周一返岗，并已将今日工作交接给汤姆。感谢理解。此致，凯文·吴
写作技巧：病假邮件要写明具体日期、简要原因、保持可联系、交接安排（hand over）。用 "come down with" 是"染上（病）"的地道表达；避免透露过多隐私，保持专业克制。

② 会议纪要 Meeting Minutes 员工健康促进计划

Meeting: Employee Wellness Program  Date: July 30, 2026  Attendees: HR, Dept. leads
1. Annual check-up uptake rose to 82%.
2. Stress survey showed 40% feel overworked.
3. Decision: launch weekly yoga and free fruit Fridays.
Action: HR to book the studio and send sign-up by Aug 5.
会议：员工健康促进计划  日期：2026年7月30日  出席：HR、各部门负责人
1. 年度体检参与率升至 82%。2. 压力调研显示 40% 感到过度工作。3. 决议：推出每周瑜伽与"周五免费水果"。行动项：HR 于 8 月 5 日前预订场地并发送报名。
写作技巧：纪要用数据说话（uptake rose to 82%）；把调研结果转化为具体决议（Decision）与责任人行动项（Action），让福祉项目可落地、可追踪。

③ 进度汇报 Progress Report 健康项目月度更新

Monthly Wellness Update — July
· Participation in fitness challenge: 145 employees (up 23%).
· Two ergonomic workshops held; 60 chairs replaced.
· Mental-health hotline usage steady at 12 calls/week.
Outlook: aim for 90% check-up coverage by Q4.
月度健康项目更新 — 7 月
· 健身挑战参与：145 人（增 23%）。· 举办两场人体工学工作坊，更换 60 把椅子。· 心理热线条用稳定，约每周 12 通。展望：第四季度前实现 90% 体检覆盖率。
写作技巧：进度汇报用动词过去式陈述已完成动作（held, replaced），用百分比量化成果（up 23%），结尾 Outlook 给下阶段目标，便于管理层评估 ROI。

④ 感谢信 Thank-you Letter 致谢医护团队

Dear Dr. Patel and team,
Thank you for the excellent care you gave me during my recovery. Your patience and clear explanations eased my anxiety and helped me heal faster. I am deeply grateful for your professionalism and kindness. Our community is lucky to have you.
With heartfelt thanks,
Helen Zhao
尊敬的帕特尔医生及团队：感谢你们在我康复期间给予的卓越照护。你们的耐心与清晰解释缓解了我的焦虑，也让我更快痊愈。我由衷感激你们的专业与善意。我们的社区有你们真是幸运。诚挚致谢，海伦·赵
写作技巧：感谢信点名对象（Dr. Patel and team）更显真诚；用具体影响（eased anxiety, heal faster）替代空泛感谢；"heartfelt thanks" 比 thanks 情感更浓，适合对医护这类高共情场景。

⑤ 道歉解释 Apology 因病缺席会议

Dear Project Team,
I sincerely apologize for missing this morning's kick-off meeting. A sudden migraine left me unable to join. I have read the minutes and will catch up with Tom on my action items. To avoid recurrence, I've rescheduled my check-up and will keep the team posted. Sorry for any inconvenience.
Sincerely,
Linda Sun
亲爱的项目团队：我为缺席今天上午的启动会深表歉意。突发偏头痛让我无法参加。我已阅读纪要，并将与汤姆对接我的行动项。为避免再发生，我已重新预约体检，并及时同步团队。对此带来的不便深表歉意。此致，孙琳达
写作技巧：道歉信 = 致歉 + 客观原因（sudden migraine，不夸大不推诿）+ 补救动作（read minutes, catch up）+ 防范措施（reschedule check-up）。用 "keep the team posted" 表持续同步，显责任感。
