实用词汇 Vocabulary

围绕职业发展场景精选 10 个高频词，点击 🔊 可听发音。

promotion
/prəˈmoʊʃn/
01
n.晋升；提拔；推广
"She earned a promotion to senior analyst after two strong quarters."
连续两个季度表现出色后，她晋升为高级分析师。
💡 记忆：pro-（向前）+ mot（移动，同 motion 运动）→ 向前移动 → 升职。注意 promote 也表示"推广产品"。
mentor
/ˈmentɔːr/
02
n./v.导师；指导者 / 指导，带教
"My mentor helped me map out a three-year development plan."
我的导师帮我规划了一份三年发展计划。
💡 记忆：源自《奥德赛》中的智者 Mentor。搭配：a mentor to sb（某人的导师）、mentorship program（导师制项目）。
competence
/ˈkɑːmpɪtəns/
03
n.能力；胜任力；专业素养
"Technical competence alone won't get you into management."
仅有技术能力并不足以让你进入管理层。
💡 记忆：compete（竞争）+ -ence → 有资格参与竞争的实力。形容词 competent（能胜任的），反义 incompetent。
appraisal
/əˈpreɪzl/
04
n.（绩效）评估；考核；评价
"Your annual appraisal is scheduled for next Thursday."
你的年度绩效评估安排在下周四。
💡 记忆：ap-（朝向）+ praise（赞扬/估价）→ 给出评价。职场固定说法：performance appraisal 绩效考核。
milestone
/ˈmaɪlstoʊn/
05
n.里程碑；重要节点
"Hitting one million users was a real milestone for the team."
用户破百万对团队而言是一个真正的里程碑。
💡 记忆：mile（英里）+ stone（石头）→ 路边的里程碑石。项目管理中常说 key milestones 关键节点。
proactive
/ˌproʊˈæktɪv/
06
adj.积极主动的；未雨绸缪的
"Be proactive—flag the risk before it becomes a problem."
要主动一些——在风险演变成问题之前就提出来。
💡 记忆：pro-（提前）+ active（行动）→ 提前行动。反义词 reactive（被动响应的），面试高频对比词。
leverage
/ˈlevərɪdʒ/
07
v./n.善用，发挥…的作用 / 杠杆，影响力
"You should leverage your finance background in this role."
你应该在这个岗位上充分发挥自己的财务背景优势。
💡 记忆：lever（杠杆）+ -age → 用小力撬动大结果。商务口语里几乎等同于 make good use of，但更显专业。
transferable
/trænsˈfɜːrəbl/
08
adj.可迁移的；可转移的
"Project management is a highly transferable skill across industries."
项目管理是一项跨行业高度通用的技能。
💡 记忆：trans-（跨越）+ fer（携带）+ -able → 能带着跨界走的。转行简历必备表达：transferable skills。
networking
/ˈnetwɜːrkɪŋ/
09
n.建立人脉；社交拓展
"Half of my job offers came through networking, not job boards."
我一半的工作机会来自人脉，而不是招聘网站。
💡 记忆：net（网）+ work（工作）→ 织自己的关系网。区分：computer networking 指计算机组网，语境不同。
resilience
/rɪˈzɪliəns/
10
n.韧性；抗压与恢复能力
"What impressed me most was her resilience after the project failed."
最让我印象深刻的是项目失败后她展现出的韧性。
💡 记忆：re-（回）+ sil（跳，同 salient）→ 被打压后弹回来。形容词 resilient，领导力评价高频词。

情景对话 Dialogues

10 组对话覆盖职场与日常生活场景，点击「朗读」可听整段对话。

职场绩效面谈：主管与下属回顾半年表现
ManagerOverall, your delivery has been solid. Where do you want to grow next?
总体而言，你的交付质量很扎实。下一步你希望在哪方面成长？
EllieI'd like to move from executing tasks to owning a workstream end to end.
我想从执行具体任务，转向端到端地负责一条工作线。
💡 own something end to end 是"全流程负责"的地道说法，比 be responsible for 更有主人翁感。
职场内部转岗：向 HR 咨询申请流程
DanielI'm interested in the internal opening on the data team. What's the process?
我对数据团队的内部空缺很感兴趣，申请流程是怎样的？
HRYou'll need your manager's sign-off first, then a panel interview next week.
你需要先拿到直属主管的签字同意，然后下周参加小组面试。
💡 sign-off = 正式批准；internal opening 指内部岗位空缺，也可说 internal vacancy。
职场导师沟通：讨论三年职业路径
SophieHonestly, I feel stuck. I've been doing the same work for two years.
说实话，我感觉卡住了，同样的工作已经做了两年。
MentorThen let's identify one stretch project that pushes you outside your comfort zone.
那我们就找一个能让你跳出舒适区的挑战性项目。
💡 feel stuck 表达职业瓶颈非常口语化；stretch project 指有难度、能拉伸能力的项目。
职场薪资谈判：候选人回应 offer 报价
RecruiterWe're offering 28K a month. How does that sound to you?
我们提供的月薪是两万八，你觉得如何？
CandidateI appreciate the offer. Based on my market research, I was hoping for something closer to 32K.
谢谢贵司的offer。根据我对市场行情的了解，我期望的数字更接近三万二。
💡 谈薪公式：先致谢 → 给依据（market research）→ 说期望。用 closer to 比直接报数字更留余地。
职场求职面试：回答"你最大的弱点"
InterviewerWhat would you say is your biggest weakness?
你认为自己最大的弱点是什么？
CandidateI used to over-polish my work. Now I set a time box and ship the draft for early feedback.
我以前会过度打磨工作成果。现在我会设定时间上限，先交出初稿获取早期反馈。
💡 弱点题黄金结构：承认问题 + 已采取的改进动作。time box（限时）和 ship（交付）都是职场高频词。
生活咖啡馆：和朋友聊要不要跳槽
KevinI keep going back and forth on whether to leave. The pay's fine, but I'm not learning.
我一直在纠结要不要离职。薪水还行，但我学不到东西。
MiaThen the real question isn't the money—it's whether you can see yourself there in three years.
那真正的问题就不是钱了，而是三年后你能否想象自己还在那里。
💡 go back and forth on sth = 反复纠结；see yourself + 地点/角色 = 想象自己处于某种状态。
职场请教同事：如何提升数据技能
NinaDo you have ten minutes? I'd love to pick your brain about learning SQL.
你有十分钟吗？我想向你请教一下学习 SQL 的经验。
AlexSure. Start with real queries on our own dashboards—it beats any tutorial.
当然。从我们自己看板上的真实查询入手，比任何教程都管用。
💡 pick someone's brain 是"请教、取经"的地道说法，礼貌又不显得低姿态。
职场申请培训预算：向总监提出请求
GraceI'd like to request budget for a certification course. It costs 6,000 yuan.
我想申请一门认证课程的预算，费用是六千元。
DirectorPut together a short business case showing how it benefits the team, and I'll approve it.
写一份简短的方案说明它对团队的价值，我就批。
💡 business case 指"投入产出论证"，申请任何资源时都可用。approve = 批准。
生活健身房偶遇前同事，聊近况
TomNo way—Lisa! What have you been up to since you left?
不会吧——Lisa！你离职之后都在忙什么呀？
LisaI switched into product design. Steep learning curve, but I've never felt more alive.
我转做产品设计了。学习曲线很陡，但我从没觉得这么有干劲。
💡 What have you been up to? 是老友重逢万能开场白；steep learning curve 指"上手很难"。
职场离职告别：与同事道别并保持联系
RyanToday's my last day. Thank you for backing me up during the audit last year.
今天是我最后一天上班。谢谢你去年审计期间对我的支持。
ColleagueIt was a pleasure. Let's stay in touch—I'll drop you a message on LinkedIn.
很荣幸和你共事。保持联系，我会在领英上给你发消息。
💡 back sb up = 支持某人；drop sb a message 比 send a message 更轻松自然。

邮件 Email申请参加行业会议
Subject: Request to Attend the 2026 Asia Product Summit Dear Ms. Chen, I would like to request approval to attend the 2026 Asia Product Summit in Shanghai on 15–16 September. The summit features three sessions directly relevant to our Q4 roadmap, particularly on AI-assisted user research. I have attached a short cost breakdown: registration is 3,200 yuan, with travel and accommodation estimated at 2,400 yuan. On my return, I will run a one-hour internal share-out so the whole team benefits from the takeaways. Please let me know if you need any further details. I would be grateful for your consideration. Best regards, Wei Zhang Product Manager
主题：申请参加 2026 亚洲产品峰会 陈女士您好： 我想申请批准参加 9 月 15 日至 16 日在上海举办的 2026 亚洲产品峰会。 本次峰会有三个议题与我们第四季度的路线图直接相关，尤其是关于 AI 辅助用户研究的部分。我已附上简要费用明细：注册费 3,200 元，差旅与住宿预计 2,400 元。 回来后，我会组织一场一小时的内部分享，让全团队都能受益于此行收获。 如需更多信息请随时告知。感谢您的考虑。 此致 张伟 产品经理
✍️ 写作技巧
  • 主题行写清"动作 + 事件 + 时间"，收件人扫一眼就知道要做什么决定。
  • 申请类邮件的说服链条：关联性（对业务的价值）→ 成本（透明预算）→ 回报（内部分享）。
  • 结尾用 I would be grateful for your consideration，比 Please approve 更得体不催促。

  • Action items 必须写成"责任人 + to do + 截止日期"三件套，否则无法追踪。

  • 开篇一行给出总体状态（On track / At risk / Delayed），让领导 3 秒抓住重点。

  • 单列 Support needed，把"求助"变成明确的一句话请求，比藏在正文里有效得多。

  • 先报好结果再致谢，让对方立刻感到自己的帮助有回报。

  • 结尾用 return the favour 提出互惠，把一次性感谢变成长期关系。

  • 四步结构：致歉 → 说明原因 → 补救方案 → 预防措施，缺一不可。
  • 解释原因后一定要接 That said / I should have...，主动担责，避免变成推卸。
  • 补救要给确定的时间点（by 6 p.m. today），模糊的 as soon as possible 只会加剧焦虑。

    BEC Vantage · Part 2
    Talent Review: Building the Promotion Shortlist
    场景：某制造企业 HR 业务伙伴与运营总监讨论晋升名单 · 约 2 分钟 · 8 句
    语速 0.9x

    📝 听力任务 Listening Tasks

    1. Why does Robert hesitate about putting Marcus forward this cycle?
    Because Marcus has only ever worked on one product line, so he lacks broader exposure — Robert wants him to lead a cross-site project first.
    因为 Marcus 只做过一条产品线，历练面不够广，Robert 希望他先牵头一个跨厂区项目。
    2. What concrete evidence does Priya use to support Amara's case?
    Amara cut changeover time by 18% and her 360-degree feedback was the strongest in the department.
    Amara 将换线时间缩短了 18%，且她的 360 度评估反馈是部门最好的。
    3. What is the agreed next step and its deadline?
    Robert will draft development plans for the two deferred candidates and send them to Priya by Friday.
    Robert 将为两位暂缓的候选人起草发展计划，并在周五前发给 Priya。

    BEC Vantage · Part 3
    One-to-One: Mapping a Career Path
    场景：软件公司团队负责人与工程师的一对一职业发展面谈 · 约 2 分钟 · 8 句
    语速 0.9x

    📝 听力任务 Listening Tasks

    1. What two career tracks does Helen describe?
    A technical track leading to principal engineer, and a people-management track leading to team lead.
    技术路线（通向首席工程师）和管理路线（通向团队负责人）。
    2. What is Jason's main concern about the management track?
    He worries his coding skills would go rusty and that he might not be able to switch back later.
    他担心编码技能会生疏，而且以后可能无法转回技术路线。
    3. What trial arrangement do they agree on?
    Jason will mentor two interns over the next quarter as a low-risk trial, then review how he feels about it.
