模块一 · 实用词汇 10 个 · 含音标 / 例句 / 记忆提示

disruptive

adj.
/dɪsˈrʌptɪv/

颠覆性的；破坏原有格局的

例句 Their disruptive technology reshaped the entire logistics industry.

记忆 dis-（分开）+ rupt（破裂，如 interrupt）→ 把旧模式“撕开”，即颠覆性。

scalability

n.
/ˌskeɪləˈbɪləti/

可扩展性；可伸缩性

例句 We need to test the system's scalability before the promotion goes live.

记忆 scale（规模）+ -ability（能力）→ 扩大规模的能力。

prototype

n.
/ˈproʊtətaɪp/

原型；雏形

例句 The engineering team built a working prototype in just two weeks.

记忆 proto-（最初）+ type（类型）→ 最初的样子 = 原型。

iterate

v.
/ˈɪtəreɪt/

迭代；重复改进

例句 We iterate on the design every sprint based on user feedback.

记忆 iter（再次，如 repeat）→ 一遍遍重做 = 迭代。

bandwidth

n.
/ˈbændwɪdθ/

带宽；也喻指（人的）精力/处理能力

例句 I don't have the bandwidth to take on another project this quarter.

记忆 band（频带）+ width（宽度）→ 原指带宽，职场常借指“精力余量”。

leverage

v.
/ˈliːvərɪdʒ/

充分利用；借助（优势/资源）

例句 We should leverage our data to improve the recommendation engine.

记忆 lever（杠杆）→ 用杠杆撬动资源 = 充分利用。

deploy

v.
/dɪˈplɔɪ/

部署；上线（软件/系统）

例句 We will deploy the new feature to production on Friday.

记忆 de-（向下）+ ploy（折叠/安排）→ 把功能“铺”到线上 = 部署。

algorithm

n.
/ˈælɡərɪðəm/

算法

例句 The ranking algorithm was updated to reduce clickbait.

记忆 源自数学家 al-Khwārizmī 的名字，泛指“计算步骤”。

bottleneck

n.
/ˈbɑːtlnek/

瓶颈；制约环节

例句 The manual review step became a bottleneck in our release pipeline.

记忆 bottle（瓶）+ neck（颈）→ 瓶口最窄处 = 瓶颈。

seamless

adj.
/ˈsiːmləs/

无缝的；流畅无感的

例句 Our goal is a seamless experience across mobile and desktop.

记忆 seam（接缝）+ -less（无）→ 没有接缝 = 无缝顺滑。

中文翻译

主题：新协作工具上线——下周一启动

团队大家好：

我们下周一将部署新的协作平台，取代旧聊天工具，提供无缝的文件共享与话题式讨论。

请于周五前备份本地重要笔记。周一 10:00 安排了 30 分钟上手培训。遇到问题可查阅帮助中心。

Liang

✍️ 写作技巧：邮件主题要一句话点明动作与时间；正文用项目符号（bullet）分点，先说结论再给细节，结尾给出下一步。

产品评审会议纪要

English

Meeting: Product Review – Search Redesign

Date: 2026-08-06 Participants: PM, Eng, Design, QA

1. The prototype demo was well received; design to iterate once more.

2. QA flagged a bottleneck in automated testing – Eng to add parallel jobs.

Next review: 2026-08-13.

中文翻译

日期：2026-08-06 参会：产品经理、研发、设计、测试

1. 原型演示反响良好，设计再迭代一版。

2. 测试指出自动化测试存在瓶颈——研发将增加并行任务。

3. 决议：下周二部署到预发，隔周五上线生产。

4. 行动项：研发修复部署脚本；设计定稿文案；测试扩充用例。

下次评审：2026-08-13。

✍️ 写作技巧：纪要用编号列表记录决议（Decision）与行动项（Action items），每条行动项必须含负责人与时限，便于追踪。

道歉信 Apology

为系统宕机致歉并解释

English

Subject: Apology for yesterday's service outage

Dear users,

We are sorry for the 40-minute outage yesterday. A faulty deploy script caused the API to reject requests. We have rolled back the change and added an extra check to prevent recurrence.

As a gesture, all paid plans get 3 days free. Thank you for your patience.

Sincerely,

Operations Team

中文翻译

主题：为昨日服务中断致歉

用户您好：

我们对昨日 40 分钟的中断深表歉意。一个有误的部署脚本导致接口拒绝请求。我们已回滚变更，并新增一道校验以防再次发生。

作为补偿，所有付费套餐赠送 3 天。感谢您的耐心。

此致，

运营团队

✍️ 写作技巧：道歉信结构：致歉 → 原因（简短、不推诿）→ 已采取的补救 → 预防措施 → 补偿。避免过多技术细节，重在责任与行动。

Listening 1 · 团队数字化转型简报

场景：部门经理在周会上向团队Briefing数字化转型项目。语速中等，含职场常用缩略与衔接。

语速 0.9

01 Good morning, everyone. Thanks for joining.

02 This quarter we are kicking off a digital transformation project. The goal is simple: make our internal tools seamless and scalable.

03 Right now, the monthly reporting process is a real bottleneck. It takes three people almost a full week.

04 We will build a small prototype first, then iterate based on your feedback. No big-bang rollout.

05 Engineering plans to deploy the first version to staging by the end of next month.

06 I don't expect this to add to your workload — we want to leverage automation to free up your time.

07 Any questions before we start? Great. Let's make this a smooth ride.

🎧 听力任务（听后作答）

  1. What is the MAIN goal of the project? (a) Cut headcount (b) Make tools seamless and scalable (c) Move office to the cloud
  2. Why is the current reporting process a problem? (a) It is too expensive (b) It takes 3 people nearly a week (c) No one owns it
  3. How will the team roll out the new system? (a) All at once (b) Prototype then iterate (c) Buy a ready-made product
查看参考答案

  • (b) It takes 3 people nearly a week
  • (b) Prototype then iterate

Listening 2 · 供应商来电：部署故障

场景：客户公司 IT 与软件供应商就一次失败的部署进行电话沟通。含问题与协商。

语速 0.9

01 Hi Sarah, this is Tom from CloudSoft support. I'm calling about the failed deploy.

02 Thanks for the quick call. Our algorithm service went down right after the update.

03 We found the issue. A config change had low bandwidth limits, so requests timed out.

04 Understood. Can you deploy a fix to our production environment today?

05 Yes. We'll push the patch this afternoon and monitor it for twenty-four hours.

06 Perfect. Please send me the post-mortem by Friday so we can avoid this bottleneck next time.

07 Will do. Sorry for the disruption, and thanks for your patience.
